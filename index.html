— HASTAREKHA SAMUDRIKA SHASTRA —

Learn What Palmistry Reveals

The lines and mounts of your palm are a mirror of your prana (life force), karma, and mental inclinations. Discover how authentic Vedic Samudrika Shastra interprets your unique hand markings.

Major Lines of the Palm
Dominant Hand

Dominant Hand vs Non-Dominant:

In Vedic Shastra, the non-dominant hand represents past karma and innate potential, while the dominant hand represents active choices, current life trajectory, and manifested destiny.

— Planetary Influences —

The Nine Vedic Hand Mounts (Parvatas)

Each elevated zone of the palm channels the vibration of a Graha (planet).

Mount of Jupiter (Guru Parvat)
Base of Index Finger

Represents spiritual wisdom, leadership, morality, administrative prestige, and social honor.

Mount of Saturn (Shani Parvat)
Base of Middle Finger

Governs discipline, endurance, solitude, karmic justice, scientific research, and long-term career goals.

Mount of Sun (Surya Parvat)
Base of Ring Finger

Bestows creativity, radiance, public popularity, artistic brilliance, executive leadership, and prosperity.

Mount of Mercury (Budha Parvat)
Base of Little Finger

Directs commercial acumen, eloquence, communication skills, diplomatic wit, and adaptability.

Mount of Venus (Shukra Parvat)
Fleshy Base of Thumb

Channels prana vitality, warmth, romantic affection, aesthetic taste, luxury, and physical resilience.

Mount of Moon (Chandra Parvat)
Lower Outer Palm

Embodies intuition, mental peace, subconscious imagination, creative writing, and foreign overseas travels.

Upper Mars (Mangal Parvat)
Outer Palm, between Mercury and Moon

Shows moral courage, patience under pressure and the ability to stand firm through difficult periods.

Lower Mars (Mangal Parvat)
Inner Palm, above the Thumb

Reflects physical courage, initiative and drive — how readily you act and defend what matters to you.

Rahu & Ketu (Central Palm)
Centre of the Palm and Above the Wrist

The shadow grahas: sudden turns of fortune, worldly ambition (Rahu) and detachment or spiritual insight (Ketu).

— Hasta Prakriti —

Types of Hands

The shape of the palm and the length of the fingers describe your basic temperament, before a single line is read.

Earth (Prithvi)
Square palm · short fingers

Practical, steady and dependable; values security and work done with the hands.

Fire (Agni)
Long palm · short fingers

Energetic, confident and quick to act; natural leaders who need a goal.

Air (Vayu)
Square palm · long fingers

Curious, articulate and analytical; thrives on ideas and communication.

Water (Jala)
Long palm · long fingers

Sensitive, intuitive and creative; feels deeply and reads people well.

Classical Types
Samudrika Shastra

Traditional texts also read conic, spatulate and mixed hands for finer detail.

— Anguli & Angushtha —

Fingers & Thumb

Each finger is ruled by a graha; the thumb shows the strength of will that steers them all.

Thumb (Angushtha)
Willpower & logic

A firm, well-set thumb shows determination; its upper joint reads will, the lower reads reasoning.

Index (Jupiter)
Ambition & self-esteem

A long index finger suggests leadership and a strong sense of purpose.

Middle (Saturn)
Responsibility

Shows discipline, seriousness and how you carry duty and long-term work.

Ring (Sun)
Creativity & recognition

Reflects artistic sense, self-expression and the wish to be seen.

Little (Mercury)
Communication

Speaks to persuasion, business sense and ease with words.
